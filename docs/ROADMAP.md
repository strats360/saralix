# Roadmap

What's next, grouped. It comes from a comparison with the popular Indian HRMS products (Keka, greytHR, Zoho People and Payroll, Darwinbox, RazorpayX Payroll, HROne, Zimyo, factoHR, Qandle, Pocket HRMS, sumHR, Spine HR) in November 2026. Shipped items move to [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Now

1. **Engagement.** Pulse surveys, polls, eNPS and kudos. *Done, in Unreleased.*
2. **Company KYC.** A new company sends its PAN, GST or other registration, address proof and an authorised signatory's ID. The platform team approves it, rejects it with a reason, or asks for more. Until it's approved the company can only do setup and KYC.
3. **Performance.** OKRs for the company, teams and people with check ins; peer and 360 feedback in review cycles; continuous feedback; 1:1 meeting notes and agendas.
4. **Paid add-ons in billing.** Add-ons on top of a plan, each with a monthly price per employee or a flat price, switched on per company and added to the invoice with GST. The four add-ons below need this first.
5. **Sign in and work tools (paid add-on).** Sign in with Google and Microsoft; Slack and Microsoft Teams alerts and approvals; an MCP server so outside AI tools can use Saralix.
6. **Zapier.** Triggers (new employee, exit, leave approved, payroll published, new kudos) and actions (add employee, add leave) on top of the public API and webhooks.

## Next

7. **Expenses and travel.** Read the amount, date and seller from a receipt photo; travel requests with approval, and advances settled against claims.
8. **Learning.** Simple courses with lessons and quizzes, assigned by department or role, with completion tracking and policy read and acknowledge.
9. **AI assistant (paid add-on).** Answers questions about leave, pay and policies, explains payslips and applies for leave. Sold per employee as an add-on to a plan, switched on per company.
10. **Face kiosk (paid add-on).** A shared tablet at the entrance that matches faces for punches.
11. **Forms and workflow builder (paid add-on).** Custom request forms with an approval workflow builder.

## On hold until we find partners

12. WhatsApp payslips and approvals (a WhatsApp Business provider), earned wage access (Jify, Jupiter or similar), group health insurance, and live salary payouts from a bank.

## Not planned for now

- Indian languages other than English.
